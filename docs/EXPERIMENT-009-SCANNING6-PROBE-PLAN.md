# Experiment 009: scanning capture development probe

The user-provided ZIP contains six scanning PCAPs. Inspect **only** `normal_scanning/normal_scanning6.pcap` for this exploratory development probe. Do not inspect or assign the other five members as final assessment captures on the basis of their names; their session independence is unknown. This capture and the already-opened ground-truth CSV are development data and are consumed for a future final-assessment purpose.

Read the requested member fully, validate its ZIP CRC and compute its uncompressed SHA-256. Inventory capture format, link type, supported transport protocols, timestamp range, packet counts and malformed/unsupported cases. Read the provided CSV fully for schema, timestamp, label and protocol summaries, without exporting addresses or ports. Timestamps in `ts` will be treated as integer epoch seconds as a hypothesis, not an externally established time-zone or duration contract.

After obtaining the PCAP's range, select the first up to 50,000 valid, distinct CSV event keys in CSV order whose timestamp is in the inclusive PCAP range. A key comprises second, protocol and directional IPv4 5-tuple. On a second full read of the same PCAP member, count selected keys with an exact same-second directional tuple match, and separately count reverse-direction matches. Record CSV duplicates, unmatched events, packet repetitions, unsupported frames and timestamp-resolution assumptions. Event-to-packet matches do not establish flow boundaries, complete ground truth or independently verified labels. Do not optimize clock offsets after inspecting matches. Do not save source identifiers in artifacts. Do not train, score, calibrate, or change the 27-feature model.

If there are no temporally eligible records or no matches, report that outcome without generalizing it to other CSVs, captures or clock offsets. Future model selection and any reserved assessment need separately recorded, non-overlapping capture roles, a frozen extractor and a label-alignment policy.
