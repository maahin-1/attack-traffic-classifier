# Experiment 009: post-hoc event-to-packet diagnostic

This exploratory diagnostic uses the already-consumed `GroundTruth_Network_1.csv` and `normal_scanning1.pcap`. Reconstruct exactly the first 50,000 distinct eligible TCP/UDP IPv4 event keys from the earlier probe, using its recorded inclusive capture range and CSV order. Verify source archive, CSV, packet member and earlier report hashes, and reproduce the previously observed **27,055** same-second matches in either direction before interpreting additional comparisons.

For those same keys only, scan the entire previously examined capture and compare tuples in either direction. Report mutually exclusive groups based on the closest category present: same second; within ±1 second but not same second; within ±5 seconds but not ±1; within ±60 seconds but not ±5; tuple observed only outside ±60 seconds; or tuple never observed in the supported packet subset. Bounds compare integer seconds, not subsecond timing. Also report selected-key transport protocols and match categories. These comparisons are diagnostic, chosen after the earlier result; they do not tune an offset, establish label correctness or flow boundaries, or estimate generalization. A key found elsewhere in the capture may be a distinct connection reusing the tuple.

Only TCP/UDP IPv4, Ethernet/Linux cooked v1 and initial fragments with sufficient headers are supported. Aggregate counts only: do not output endpoints, ports, per-event rows or payloads. Do not use unmatched events as evidence of benign traffic. No model fitting, threshold calibration, holdout selection, or scoring is part of this diagnostic. Preserve other captures and labels outside this development sample.
