For this separate exploratory check, re-create the exact same first 50,000 distinct event keys, use only the already-consumed scanning1 member, and reproduce the earlier same-second and adjacent-only totals. Scan complete packets and compare source event integer seconds to original PCAP seconds and microsecond fractions without fitting a clock offset.

For adjacent-only keys, report whether a tuple-matching packet occurs in the prior PCAP second, the next PCAP second, or both. For prior-second matches, report whether at least one matched packet has a fractional second at or above 0.5, below 0.5, or both; analogous groupings may overlap. A ceiling or nearest-second interpretation is a hypothesis, not proof of the publisher's timestamp conversion or source-flow identity.
